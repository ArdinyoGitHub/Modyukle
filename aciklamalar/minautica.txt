# Minautica

An unofficial, fan-made mod inspired by **Subnautica**. Travel through the portal to **planet 4546B**, an ocean world.

## Planet 4546B
- A whole new dimension of clear, fog-free ocean
- Biomes follow depth bands like the original: **Safe Shallows** (down to 20 m) and **Kelp Forest** (down to 100 m)
- Get there through the **4546B Portal**

## Seafloor
Coral, Table Coral, limestone and sandstone outcrops, quartz, lithium, acid mushrooms, blood kelp, blue palms,
veined nettle, writhing weed and many types of sand and moss.

## Creatures
- **Crashfish**: be careful, they explode on contact

## Vehicles
- **Seamoth**
- **Cyclops**

## Materials
Titanium, copper, silver, gold, lead, lithium, quartz and crafting parts like wiring kits, computer chips,
batteries and power cells.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
