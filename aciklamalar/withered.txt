# Wither Legacy

Opens the way to the **Wither Dimension**, a dark land where the Wither's power grows out of the ground.

## Wither Dimension
- Three biomes: **Wither**, **Blue Wither** and **Purple Wither**
- Wither Soil in three colors and Wither Stone
- **Wither Castles** and **Wither Towers** to explore
- Open the portal with the Witherdimension Portal Igniter

## Resources
- **Nether Star Ore**: mine Nether Stars without fighting the Wither
- Wither plants and the **Wither Fruit**
- Nether Stick

## Gear
- Wither Sword, Pickaxe, Axe and Shovel
- Bıçak (knife)
- Full Wither armor set

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
