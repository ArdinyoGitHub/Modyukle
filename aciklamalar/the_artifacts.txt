# The Artifacts

Deep in the caves grow two glowing crystals. Walk close to one and it drives itself into your chest,
giving you its power for good. Each player can hold only **one** artifact, so choose wisely.

## Green Artifact: Time
- **[V] Stop time** for 15 seconds (30 second cooldown). You can move freely. You also take no fall damage while time is stopped.
- **Electric shock**: creatures you touch or hit are shocked
- **Charged punch**: hold **[G]** with an empty hand to charge a critical punch

## Pink Artifact: Charm
- **[V]** For 5 seconds your fists glow pink. Every creature you punch **joins your side** (the punch deals no damage)
- Allies follow you and defend you. Tameable animals like wolves, cats and horses become real pets
- You regenerate rapidly, and so do nearby allies
- Bosses resist your influence

## Saved from death
Both artifacts work like a **Totem of Undying**, once. When you would die, the crystal shatters and saves you.
After that its power is gone, and you can find a new crystal.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
