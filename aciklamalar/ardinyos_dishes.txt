# Ardinyo's Dishes

Adds 16 new foods to Minecraft, inspired by home cooking and Turkish street food. Every dish has a crafting recipe.

## Cakes
- Apple Cake
- Sweet Berry Cake
- Carrot Cake
- Golden Carrot Cake

## Sweets
- Chocolate and Melted Chocolate
- Kaymak (Turkish clotted cream)
- Bread with Kaymak and Honey

## Street food
- **Balık Ekmek**: the famous Istanbul fish sandwich
- Sandwiches with fries, chicken, meatballs (köfte), mutton, pork or rabbit
- Chicken Nuggets

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
