# Extended Sculks

Takes the sculk of the Deep Dark much further: new materials, tools, creatures and a whole sculk dimension.

## Sculk Dimension
A dark dimension with two biomes, **JES** and the **Sculk Forest**, full of sculk trees and lakes of **Sculk Fluid**.
Open the portal with the **Sculk Dimension Portal Igniter**.

## Creatures
14 new mobs, including the Sculk Demon, Aura of the Dark, Soul Blaster, Lurker, Sculk Monster, Sculk Owl,
Ender Sculk, Depth, Sculk Head, Warden Biped and Wayfinder.

## Gear
- **Sculk Ingot**, Catalystick and Integrity Fragment
- Sculk Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe and a Sculk Combat Axe
- **Hard Sculk** block

## Sculk Sickness
A new status effect. Be careful around the sculk.

All items are in their own **Sculks** creative tab.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
