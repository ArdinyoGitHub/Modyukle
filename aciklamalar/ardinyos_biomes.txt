# Ardinyo's Biomes

Adds five colorful biomes, a new dimension and nine new wood types to Minecraft.

## Biomes
- **Violet**: purple skies, amethyst clusters, chorus patches and crying obsidian
- **Crimson**: red forests with lava lakes and striders
- **Emerald**: emerald veins, slime pools, mossy ruins and frogs
- **Midas**: golden trees, dry grass, camels and ocelots
- **Allay**: frosty blue forests with extra lapis and diamond ore, allays and pitcher plants

The biomes generate in the Overworld (via TerraBlender) and in the **Prism Dimension**.

## Prism Dimension
Build a portal frame out of **Block of Paradise** and light it with the **Prism Dimension Portal Igniter**.
Paradise Ore is mined to craft the frame blocks.

## Wood types
Cherry, Crimson, Dust, Emeralld, Ender, Midas, Midnight, Skyface and Sunny, each with logs, wood, planks,
leaves, stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, buttons and pressure plates.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
- [TerraBlender](https://modrinth.com/mod/terrablender)
