# MCSTRAY

An unofficial, fan-made mod inspired by **Stray**. Bring the robot city and its dangers into Minecraft.

## Walled City 99
Every world hides one huge underground city, sealed under a concrete dome. It is an original city inspired by the real Kowloon Walled City (not a copy of the game's map).
- **Midtown**: the upper level, with streets, plazas and neon lights
- **The Slums**: the lower level right along the great wall
- **Dead City**: the rest of the lower level, overrun by Zurgs, with a huge Zurg-infested tower at its heart
- Get in through the hole at the top of the dome
- Broken robots lie around the city, waiting to be found

## Finding the City
- Villages may have a **Sweeper 457** robot. Talk to it to get a **Distress Receiver**
- The receiver points to the Walled City and beeps faster as you get closer
- Inside the city, it leads you to the nearest broken robot

## B-12
Your smart drone companion.
- Bring a **Broken Robot** to Doc to have it repaired into B-12
- B-12 follows you and teleports to you if it falls behind
- B-12 has its own inventory. Only its owner can open it or interact with it
- Sneak + right-click to pick B-12 back up. Anything it carries drops on the ground
- Doc can fit B-12 with a **Defluxor**. B-12 can then shine UV light on Zurgs and defend you

## Robots
- **Doc**: runs a shop (Defluxor, B-12 repair, B-12 Defluxor upgrade) and fights Zurgs with his own Defluxor
- **Seamus** and **Momo**: friendly robots
- **Sentinels**: security robots that fire at intruders

## Weapons
- **Defluxor**: hold right-click to cast a wide UV light on floors, walls and ceilings. Zurgs caught in it are wiped out, and undead burn
- **Sentinel Blaster**: sometimes dropped by Sentinels. A long-range stun gun: its electric beam stuns the target for a few seconds without knocking it back

## Zurgs
- Swarming **Zurgs** that hatch from **Zurg Eggs** and spread the **Zurg Virus**. Some of them are small
- They attack anything that is not a Zurg, and only fear light
- Zurg Flesh can be cooked (or burnt)

## Zurg Dimension
A dimension infested with Zurgs, with three Zurgland biomes. Open the portal with the Zurgdimesion Portal Igniter.

All items are in the **Stray** creative tab.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.3
