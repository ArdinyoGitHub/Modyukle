# MCHOL

An unofficial, fan-made mod inspired by **High on Life**. Become an intergalactic bounty hunter in Minecraft.

## Guns
Kenny, Gus, Sweezy and Creature, plus alternate forms like Multiglob Kenny, Goop Suck Gus and Portal Sweezy.
Each one fires its own projectiles.

## Bounty Hunter gear
- Full **Bounty Hunter armor** set with a **jetpack** (bound to its own key)
- **BH9K** teleporter: open its menu to travel between the Overworld, the Nether, the End and the alien worlds

## Alien worlds
- **Zephyr Paradise**: two biomes, Zephyr trees, villages with their own Zephyr villager profession
- **Gatlus** and **Terenne**
- Blue Grass, Skyflower and Flameflower plants

## Building
Two new wood types, **Zephyra** and **Zephyra Blue**, with planks, stairs, slabs, fences, doors, trapdoors,
signs, hanging signs, boats and chest boats. Plus Zephyr stone and cobblestone.

## Creatures
Merk and Blue Myte.

All items are in the **High On Life** creative tab.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
