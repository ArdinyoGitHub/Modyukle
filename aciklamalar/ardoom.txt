# MCDOOM (ardimastered)

A big Doom mod for Minecraft. It combines my own Doom mod (LQDM) with **MCDoom by AzureDoom**, updated to Minecraft 26.1.2.

## Demons
Around 50 demons in four tiers:
- **Fodder**: Imp, Zombieman, Possessed Soldier, Shotgun Guy, Chaingunner, Lost Soul, Gargoyle, Mecha Zombie...
- **Heavy**: Cacodemon, Pinky, Revenant, Mancubus, Hell Knight, Arachnotron, Pain Elemental, Whiplash...
- **Super heavy**: Baron of Hell, Cyberdemon, Marauder, Arch-Vile, Doom Hunter, Summoner...
- **Bosses**: Icon of Sin, Spider Mastermind, Gladiator, Mother Demon, Arch Maykr

## Weapons
- MCDoom's 3D animated guns (shotguns, BFG, chaingun, plasma rifle, rocket launcher, Unmaykr and more), crafted at the **Gun Table**
- **Crucible** gear: swords, blades, axes, halberds and a pickaxe
- Chainsaws, the Sentinel Hammer, the Doom Blade and the Marauder Axe
- Guitars: Crucible, Hell, Slayer Tech, Cultist, Maykr and Ancient Sentinel
- Eternal Pistol and Toxic Shooter
- Meathook

## Armor
Doom Slayer armor sets including Classic Doom Marine, Midnight, Praetor Crimson and many MCDoom skins.

## Doom Hell
A hellish dimension full of demons. Use `/doomhell` to travel there and back.

## Also included
Doom ore, Argent energy, demon meat, villager trades, chest loot and music discs.

## Requirements
- NeoForge for Minecraft 26.1.2
- [AzureLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/azurelib)
- [Common Networking](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/common-networking) (by Jaredlll08)
- [SmartBrainLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/smartbrainlib)
